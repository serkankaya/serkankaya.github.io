Gatewarden — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Serkan Kaya

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Gatewarden collects, how it is used, with whom it is shared and how you can delete it. Contact: serkankayatr212@gmail.com.

1. Information collected

  • Game progress: your save (waves, buildings, resources, upgrades, settings) and language preference are stored only on your device.
  • Advertising data: optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction events.
  • Cloud save (optional): if you sign in to Google Play Games Services, your player ID and a snapshot of your save are stored in your Google account. Sign-in is not required to play.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or health data. The Game has no accounts and no in-app purchases.

2. How information is used

  • To run the Game and save your progress.
  • To show and measure ads, apply frequency limits and prevent ad fraud.
  • To restore your progress on another device when you use cloud save.

3. Sharing

  • Google — AdMob for advertising and Google Play Games Services for sign-in and cloud save. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Authorities — only where required by law.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local save data is kept until you uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Cloud save data is kept until you delete it from the Play Games app or your Google account at myaccount.google.com.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and change your ad consent choice in the Game's settings.
  • For any other request, email serkankayatr212@gmail.com.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child's data has been processed, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
